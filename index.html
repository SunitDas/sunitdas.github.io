Sunit Das

FARE Fellow, Department of Physics, IIT Kanpur

I am a theoretical condensed matter physicist working on quantum geometry and the transport and optical phenomena it generates in crystalline materials. I completed my Ph.D. at IIT Kanpur under the supervision of Prof. Amit Agarwal, and now continue this work there as a FARE Fellow.

Research Directions

My work spans six connected themes — see the Research page for the full descriptions.

Chiral anomalies in Weyl systems

Electrical, thermal, and gravitational chiral anomalies, and nonlinear magnetotransport in tilted Weyl and Kramers-Weyl metals.

Hidden planar band geometry

Planar and magnetoelectric Hall effects sourced by hidden planar and layer-orbital quantum geometry in layered materials.

Probing broken time-reversal symmetry

Odd-parity magnetoresistance as a transport fingerprint of intrinsic TRS breaking where the anomalous Hall effect is forbidden.

Aperiodic quantum oscillations

Generalized Onsager quantization, nonlinear Landau fan diagrams, and magnetic-field-induced Fermi surface expansion.

Quantum-geometric spintronics

Nonlinear pure spin Hall effect, light-induced spin magnetization, and deterministic switching in altermagnets.

Valleytronics & strain gauge fields

Transport spectroscopy of pseudomagnetic fields in graphene and the broader 2D valleytronics landscape.
