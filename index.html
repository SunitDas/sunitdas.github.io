Sunit Das

FARE Fellow, Department of Physics, IIT Kanpur

Quantum Geometry · Quantum Transport · Nonlinear Response · Quantum Materials

I am a theoretical condensed matter physicist working on quantum geometry-driven charge, spin, and orbital transport and optical phenomena in quantum materials. I completed my Ph.D. at IIT Kanpur, India, under the supervision of Prof. Amit Agarwal, and I am continuing there as a FARE Fellow.
